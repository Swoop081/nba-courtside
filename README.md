# NBA Courtside — v0.59 Games, Stats & News First

**v0.59 supersedes v0.58 as the working development baseline.** It retains the dedicated incoming-trade proposal review from v0.58, the casual-first hierarchy from v0.57, and the continuous real draft pipeline from v0.56. The unfinished v0.53 long-save certification branch remains separate and unpromoted.

## v0.59 Home-loop changes

- Retires the daily/off-day Training Center from the playable franchise loop. Team Practice, Shooting Work, Defensive Install, Recovery Day, Film Session and Young Player Focus are no longer surfaced as daily choices.
- Training no longer occupies Home, no longer appears as the off-day status, and no longer competes with the next game for first-viewport attention.
- Existing historical daily-training records remain save-compatible, but v0.59 does not expose or create new daily plans.
- Keeps required GM actions and urgent medical/team alerts above entertainment content.
- Places the current/next game immediately after required actions/alerts.
- Moves the most engaging league information directly under the game: latest league digest, SportsCenter performance/stat coverage, League Wire, then NBA Today headlines.
- Retains deeper franchise, staff, development, G League, draft-watch and career systems below the casual-first layer.
- Save schema remains 25.

See `docs/HIERARCHY_V59.md` and `docs/VALIDATION_V59.md`.
