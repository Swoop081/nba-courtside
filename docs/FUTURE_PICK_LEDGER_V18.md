# NBA Courtside v0.18 — Future Pick Ledger

Freeze date: **20 August 2026**

v0.18 replaces the old assumption that every future draft slot can be represented as a simple tradeable `team owns team’s pick` object.

The new ledger is an **origin-right safety ledger** for 2027–2033.

## Coverage

The source file `data/future-pick-ledger-2026-08-20.json` contains exactly:

- **30 teams**
- **7 draft years** — 2027 through 2033
- **2 rounds**
- **420 unique origin cells**

Every cell identifies the original team, year, round, current executable owner where safely known, tradeability, source status, and an explanatory summary.

### Current status split

- **197 tradeable/source-checked cells**
- **223 complex, frozen, or source-locked cells**

A source-locked cell remains visible as draft capital context but cannot be included in a trade package.

## Direct obligations and protections

Simple obligations are represented as actual ownership and therefore can participate in the GM asset system. Examples encoded in the safety ledger include direct current ownership for selected future firsts such as:

- Atlanta 2027 first → San Antonio
- New York 2027 first → Brooklyn
- Phoenix 2027 first → Houston
- New York 2029 first → Brooklyn
- Toronto 2031 first → LA Clippers

The 2027 resolver also retains explicit protection logic for cases encoded in the current model, including:

- Dallas retains No. 1–2; otherwise Charlotte receives the pick.
- Lakers retain No. 1–4; otherwise Memphis receives the pick.
- Miami retains No. 1–14; otherwise Charlotte receives the pick.

Protection labels flow into the player-facing draft-capital UI.

## Complex rights are not flattened into fake ownership

Multi-team favorable/less-favorable swaps and other conditional chains can depend on the final draft order. A single preseason `owner` field cannot faithfully describe those rights.

v0.18 therefore uses statuses such as:

- `complex`
- `frozen`
- `source_locked`

and sets `tradeable:false` when the right is not safe to expose as an ordinary movable asset.

This is deliberately conservative. A nontradeable source-locked cell is preferable to letting the user trade a pick the franchise may not legally own.

## 2027 draft settlement

The existing 2027 first-round draft resolver remains active for the linked rights already implemented in code. It applies final draft position before resolving the current modeled protection/swap relationships.

The source ledger and draft resolver serve different purposes:

**Ledger:** what can safely appear and be traded before the draft.

**Resolver:** who actually selects once final draft positions make a conditional right settleable.

## Save migration safety

`upgradeDraftAssetsV18()` applies two rules:

1. If an old save still shows an untouched origin owner but the certified ledger has a different current owner, v0.18 repairs that ownership.
2. If the user has already traded that asset to a third franchise inside their universe, v0.18 preserves the user's alternate-history transaction rather than overwriting it with the real-world starting owner.

Certified locks/protection metadata are then merged into the migrated asset.

## Future horizon

For 2027–2033, `ensureFuturePickHorizon()` prefers the source-certified cell.

Years beyond the sourced window can still be generated by the endless franchise universe. Those are explicitly tagged as **future-generated** own picks, because by then they belong to the alternate-history simulation rather than the frozen 2026 real-world asset ledger.

## Stepien guard

The existing seven-year Stepien smoke guard remains active. A trade that would leave a club without first-round capital in consecutive future drafts is blocked by the prototype transaction engine.

The full modern CBA contains specialist nuances that remain part of the broader CBA certification roadmap; v0.18 does not claim the draft-asset implementation is a legal-opinion engine.

## What this ledger is — and is not

### It is
- complete **origin-cell coverage** for 2027–2033;
- a safe player-facing inventory of future draft rights;
- executable for simple direct/protected rights currently modeled;
- a guard against fabricated tradeable assets;
- migration-aware.

### It is not yet
- a fully executable graph for every nested favorable/less-favorable swap;
- a complete line-by-line settlement engine for every second-round conditional conveyance;
- a substitute for future updates when real transactions change the league after the freeze date.

That boundary is why the phrase **origin-right safety ledger** is used throughout v0.18 rather than “fully solved future pick tree.”
