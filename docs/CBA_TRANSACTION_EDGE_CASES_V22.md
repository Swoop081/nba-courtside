# NBA Courtside v0.22 — Specialist CBA + Transaction Edge Cases

## Purpose

v0.22 closes the specialist transaction cases most likely to change a GM decision without claiming that every clause in the long-form CBA is represented. The implementation follows NBA CBA 101 / the 2023 NBA-NBPA CBA and the official 2026-27 cap release.

## Traded Player Exceptions

Courtside now stores a persistent TPE object with team, original amount, remaining amount, creation date, one-year expiry, source player and source type. A TPE may be partially consumed and cannot be combined with ordinary outgoing salary as a fake aggregated exception.

Salary matching follows the current apron table:
- Standard TPE: 100% outgoing + $250,000, with the $250,000 reduced to zero when post-trade Team Salary exceeds the First Apron.
- Aggregated Standard TPE: simultaneous aggregation, Second Apron constraint.
- Expanded TPE: current CBA formula, First Apron constraint.
- Pre-existing Standard TPE generated in a prior salary-cap year: First Apron transaction.
- TPE generated by a sign-and-trade: Second Apron transaction when used.

A conservative residual TPE is generated when a qualifying over-cap club sends one salary asset and takes back less salary. The save records creation, partial use and expiry.

## Exception acquisition by trade

Beginning with 2024-25, the Bi-Annual Exception, Non-Taxpayer MLE and Room MLE may be used to acquire players by trade or waiver claim. v0.22 activates those trade-acquisition paths and consumes the same exception ledger used for free-agent signings. The NTMLE and BAE routes carry the First Apron transaction restriction; the Room MLE route is limited to teams already classified as room teams. The Taxpayer MLE is intentionally **not** a trade-acquisition route.

The Minimum Salary Exception can acquire a player signed to a one- or two-year minimum contract. Courtside supports this prospectively when the incoming contract was created by the in-save minimum route; it does not guess that an unsourced starting contract was a minimum-exception contract.

## Sign-and-trade + BYC

A team's own free-agent rights can appear as a Sign-and-Trade asset before the regular season begins. The generated contract uses:
- 3 years in the current UI (CBA range 3–4);
- at least three non-option years;
- fully guaranteed first year;
- 5% annual raises;
- only Bird/Early Bird/Non-Bird signing authority, never NTMLE or Room MLE.

The receiving team becomes hard-capped at the First Apron. A team that already used the Taxpayer MLE cannot receive a sign-and-traded player because that would add a First Apron transaction after a Second Apron transaction route that blocks it.

When the assignor is over the cap, uses Bird/Early Bird authority and the new first salary exceeds the Non-Bird maximum, BYC matching salary becomes the greater of the prior salary and 50% of the first-year sign-and-trade salary. The receiver still counts the full actual salary.

## Extension-and-trade

The safe extension-and-trade envelope is:
- first extended-year salary no greater than 120% of the greater of final original-term salary and estimated average player salary;
- annual raises/decreases no greater than 5%;
- no more than four total contract years including years remaining on the original term.

A richer, faster-growing or longer ordinary extension creates a six-month trade lock. A player acquired within the prior six months may only sign an extension inside the safe envelope during that window.

## Contract salary math

Veteran contract raises/decreases are calculated as a percentage of the **first-year salary**, not compounded from the prior contract year. For example, an 8% four-year deal starting at $10M produces $10.0M / $10.8M / $11.6M / $12.4M. The general maximum salary calculation also respects the rule that 105% of the player's prior-season salary may exceed the ordinary 25% / 30% / 35% service-tier maximum. Current-player tier selection still inherits the explicit exact-YOS boundary described below.

## Transaction timing

Forward-generated contracts now receive explicit trade locks:
- draft rookie: 30 days after signing;
- standard free-agent signing: later of three months after signing or Dec. 15;
- Bird/Early Bird signing above 120% of prior salary while the club is over the cap (unless minimum): later of three months or Jan. 15.

A sign-and-traded player is exempt for the initial sign-and-trade itself and receives the normal new-signing lock after arriving at the new team.

A contract acquired by trade using an Exception cannot have its salary aggregated in a subsequent trade for two months. If it was acquired on or before December 16, Courtside applies the CBA's day-before/deadline-day carve-out. A player may still be re-traded alone when no separate trade restriction applies. CPU-to-CPU transactions use the same eligibility and aggregation checks.

**Starting-history boundary:** the frozen August 2026 roster does not contain a complete certified signing-date history. v0.22 therefore does not guess historical lock dates for starting players. Every transaction created inside the save is timed exactly from its in-save date.

## One-year Bird consent

A player on a one-year Bird/Early Bird contract who would retain qualifying rights at the end of the contract requires player consent before a trade. Courtside makes the player decision deterministically from role/winning preference and morale. If the player consents and is traded, Bird continuity is reset as required by the special one-year-contract rule.

## Reacquisition restriction

When a team trades a player, the save creates a free-agent reacquisition lock for the assignor. The lock expires at the earlier of:
- one year from the trade; or
- July 1 following the last non-option season of the contract.

This v0.22 implementation blocks waiver/free-agent reacquisition. More exotic multi-team assignment sequencing remains a future specialist case.

## High-salary waived players

If a player is waived during the regular season and his pre-waiver salary exceeded the NTMLE, a new signing is a First Apron transaction. A club cannot complete that signing above the First Apron, and a club that has already used the Taxpayer MLE cannot use that First Apron route.

## Rookie contracts + minimum salary

First-round generated picks now use 120% of the cap-scaled Rookie Salary Scale, two guaranteed seasons and separate team options in Years 3 and 4.

All second-round generated picks use a four-year Second Round Pick Exception contract. This keeps the existing four-year rookie-contract franchise interface while using the correct second-round exception salary structure instead of a first-round approximation. When the contract is signed before July 31, its salary is excluded from Team Salary until July 31.

The official 2024-25 Minimum Annual Salary Scale and Second Round Pick Exception table are stored in `cba.js` and rise with the Salary Cap as prescribed. One- and two-year minimum contracts use the official minimum table for each contract year rather than a generic percentage raise. Generated players carry exact in-save service years. Exact current-player YOS is not present in the frozen starting source and therefore remains a clearly marked modeled input until certified.

## Deliberate boundaries after v0.22

Not claimed exact yet:
- current-player exact YOS seed;
- full Apron Team Salary adjustments (Courtside currently uses its Team Salary proxy for transaction gates, so incentive/hold-specific apron adjustments are not claimed exact);
- acquisition-by-trade under the Minimum Salary Exception for unsourced starting contracts (prospective in-save minimum contracts are supported);
- unlikely/likely incentive accounting;
- signing/trade bonuses;
- partial guarantees and guarantee triggers;
- set-off and stretch elections;
- cash consideration ledger;
- full waiver-claim priority/assignment mechanics;
- Two-Way / Exhibit 10 transactions;
- Disabled Player Exception;
- every designated-veteran and rookie-extension eligibility edge case;
- direct same-season trade reacquisition beyond the implemented waiver/free-agent reacquisition lock;
- claim-level trading of the v0.21 linked draft-right objects.

The project rule remains: uncertified inputs are exposed as boundaries instead of silently invented.
