# v0.8 — League Pulse

League Pulse is generated entirely from the current save. It does not inject fixed season narratives.

Implemented:
- Home Your Form card: Last 10, streak, point differential and home record.
- Around the League image-heavy story modules generated from completed games.
- Pulse page: featured headlines, live MVP race, hottest teams, player trends and quick leaders.
- Leaders: PTS, REB, AST, STL, BLK, FG%, 3PT%, FT% with sample/attempt qualification.
- Awards: live MVP, DPOY, ROY, Sixth Man and Most Improved ladders.
- Story/trend detection for big nights, win streaks, rookie surges, breakouts, hot scoring runs and veteran production changes.

All content derives from results, seasonStats, gameLogs, current rosters and standings. Live races are indicative until final awards are generated after Game 82.
