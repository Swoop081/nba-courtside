# NBA Courtside v0.47 — League History + Long-Term World Persistence

## Goal

v0.47 makes the NBA Courtside league universe remember its own simulated history across seasons and across the user GM's employment changes. It builds on the existing `state.history` champion/award ledger and does not replace the schema-25 save model.

## Permanent season archive

Before a completed season can be reset into the offseason, v0.47 stores a detailed snapshot in `state.leagueHistoryV47.seasons`:

- NBA champion and Finals MVP
- season awards
- East and West final standings
- all 30 team records
- end-of-season team roster, payroll and roster-power snapshots
- regular-season player statistical totals for every player with a game played
- postseason bracket/state and postseason game results
- season transaction snapshot

The archive is created before the v0.46 employment review / v0.45 offseason transition can clear season statistics. Saves upgraded while already paused at the employment-review or team-options checkpoint are also captured before reset when the detailed season data is still available.

## Upgrade behavior

Existing completed seasons in the older `state.history` collection are imported lazily as **legacy season records**. Their known champion, Finals MVP, awards and team records remain visible. v0.47 does not fabricate detailed standings/player season totals for seasons whose granular data had already been discarded by an older build.

## League History workspace

The existing League → History destination expands into five views:

1. **Overview** — latest champion, save-era record book and championship ledger.
2. **Seasons** — one archive per completed season with awards, standings and retirements.
3. **Franchises** — all 30 teams accumulate save-era titles, Finals appearances, best records, draft history and user-GM eras.
4. **Players** — career leaders from archived NBA Courtside seasons plus a persistent retirement directory.
5. **Drafts** — archived selections by draft year, including current owner and original pick source.

All records are explicitly save-era/simulated-universe records. They are not presented as claims about real NBA history.

## Player careers

Every archived season with a player appearance contributes to that player's NBA Courtside career totals. Player sheets gain a Career History section listing archived seasons, team, games and per-game scoring/rebounding/assists. Retired players remain inspectable through the history directory.

## Drafts and retirements

Draft selections are copied into `state.leagueHistoryV47.drafts` while the draft state still exists. Retirements generated by the existing offseason development/retirement model are attached both to the completed season and to a league retirement ledger.

## GM career continuity

League history is stored at the league save level, not the controlled-team level. Accepting a new job in v0.46 therefore leaves all archived champions, records, drafts, retired players and former-franchise seasons untouched.

## Compatibility

- Formal save schema remains `25`.
- Save key remains `nbaCourtsideSaveV25`.
- New state is additive under `state.leagueHistoryV47`.
- v0.46 GM Career, v0.45 Offseason Command Center, v0.44 Trade Deadline, v0.44.1 Primary Action Hierarchy, v0.43 Franchise Direction, Game Day, CBA and all retained league systems remain authoritative.
