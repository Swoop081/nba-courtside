# v0.55 — 2018–19 Opening Night + Real 2019 Draft

## Product rule

**History diverges before Game 1 on October 16, 2018.** Opening-night facts establish the universe. Everything after that point — results, trades, standings, awards, playoff outcomes, development, contracts, coaching moves and draft destinations — belongs to the save.

## Source-backed opening state

### Rosters

The official NBA opening-night roster release dated October 15, 2018 is the roster authority. The pack contains 494 player entries across all 30 teams. Original active/inactive/suspended/two-way status is retained in historical metadata; exact injury-duration reconstruction is not yet part of this pack.

### Schedule

The NBA's official schedule release establishes an October 16, 2018 opening and April 10, 2019 regular-season end across 1,230 games. The playable pack contains all 1,230 matchup/date records. Scores are intentionally absent because the divergence point precedes Game 1.

### Ratings

The pack uses day-one 2018–19 DARKO evidence as its primary no-hindsight rating backbone where a clean identity match exists. It resolves 478 of 494 opening-roster players. Prior-season statistical evidence and conservative model fallback are used where day-one evidence does not resolve.

This is deliberately different from using a player's later NBA career to decide how good they were in October 2018. For example, Luka Doncic enters the 2018 universe as a high-upside young player rather than being assigned his later superstar level by hindsight.

### Salary / CBA environment

2018–19 opening salary evidence resolves 447 of 494 roster identities. The league uses the official 2018–19 salary cap ($101.869M), luxury-tax line ($123.733M) and salary floor ($91.682M), with a 2017-CBA-era tax-apron model. Modern second-apron restrictions are disabled for this start.

Exact unresolved contract-year continuity and detailed historical transaction-rule edge cases remain explicitly modeled until dedicated certification packs replace them.

## Real 2019 rookie pipeline

The 2019 Draft pool contains all 60 official drafted identities in historical pick order. Official pick/team is retained as source metadata only.

The runtime explicitly verifies that Zion Williamson can be drafted No. 1 by Boston in an alternate universe while his historical metadata continues to record New Orleans as his real-world destination.

The scouting engine remains authoritative for the alternate universe. Real draft order is evidence, not an instruction to replay the real career.

## Pipeline coverage

- **2019:** complete official 60-player draft-result identity class.
- **2020–2025:** generated classes for now; these are the next historical data-expansion target and are not represented as real classes.
- **2026:** retained complete official 60-player draft-result identity class from v0.54.
- **2027:** retained source-backed prospect watch class.
- Other uncovered years: generated until a certified real-class pack is added.

## Known modeled boundaries

- traded future-pick ownership at the 2018 divergence point;
- unresolved contract years beyond sourced salary evidence;
- exact opening-day injury durations;
- 2018–19 coaching/staff identities;
- some deep 2017-CBA transaction edge behavior;
- era-specific logos/visual branding.

These boundaries are surfaced in the game and should not be interpreted as exact historical facts.
