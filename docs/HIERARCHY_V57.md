# v0.57 — Casual-First Information Hierarchy + Advance Clarity

## Home hierarchy

1. **Primary action** — play, resolve a blocker, or advance the league.
2. **Essential context** — needs-attention items, next game, today/training, last-advance digest, league wire.
3. **Broad context** — daily feature, league events and headline/score coverage.
4. **Franchise deep dive** — player relations, health systems, staff, G League, draft watch, GM evaluation/career and Development Watch.

The purpose is not to remove simulation depth. It is to make that depth opt-in through scrolling rather than forcing it into the first viewport.

## Off-day progression

Training is optional. It can provide a small benefit, but the calendar is not gated by selecting a plan. The primary `Advance 1 Day` control remains available whenever there is no actual blocking GM decision.

## Mobile presentation

On iPhone-sized screens the daily advance control becomes a full-width, high-contrast button inside the first action card. The next opponent is summarized immediately below it on off days.
