# NBA Courtside v0.44 — Trade Deadline + Transaction Presentation

## Purpose
v0.44 turns the existing CBA-certified trade engine and active-front-office model into a playable transaction-market experience. It does not replace trade legality, player value foundations, save schema, roster rules or the v0.43 GM evaluation system.

## Trade Deadline Command Center
The command center appears during the 30 days before the in-save trade deadline and becomes the primary market surface on deadline day. It exposes:
- days to the official in-save deadline;
- simulated market heat;
- the user's current Buyer / Seller / Re-tooling / Standing Pat stance;
- top roster needs, tradeable first-round inventory, payroll state and incoming-call count;
- a live simulated league intelligence board.

The 2026–27 starting universe uses the schedule-certified deadline of **11 February 2027**. Future seasons continue to use the shifted schedule configuration already generated by the franchise engine.

## Playable Deadline Day
Deadline Day is no longer a normal date that seven-day or next-game simulation can silently jump over.

The day contains four market windows:
1. Morning Calls
2. Midday Market
3. Final Calls
4. Closing Bell

Advance Day advances one market window while the date remains fixed. Existing GM Action Center blockers remain authoritative. After Closing Bell, the next advance closes the market and proceeds to the next league day.

`SIM 7 DAYS` and `SIM TO NEXT GAME` stop at Deadline Day when their normal destination would pass it.

## Dynamic Team Trade Stances
Every CPU club derives a live market stance from its current record, roster power, age curve, injuries, payroll and existing front-office model:
- **Buyer** — prioritizes a present-day upgrade.
- **Seller** — prioritizes future value and flexibility.
- **Re-tooling** — willing to reshape the roster while remaining competitive.
- **Standing Pat** — open to value without forcing a major change.

These stances are gameplay simulation and can change as the season changes.

## Dynamic Trade Philosophies
CPU teams also expose a simulated transaction philosophy:
- **Contender** — immediate impact, playoff fit and depth.
- **Asset Collector** — draft capital, youth and control years.
- **Cap Manager** — flexibility, efficient money and optionality.
- **Core Protector** — continuity and a high bar for foundational-player moves.

The philosophy modifies presentation and deadline valuation pressure within bounded limits. It does not bypass the CBA engine or create a second legality system.

## Market Interest + Rumours
Interest is presented as Cold / Exploratory / Interested / Motivated / Aggressive. It responds to front-office aggression, current stance, roster needs, player fit and deadline proximity.

League-intelligence copy is explicitly labeled **simulated**. It is generated from the current save state and is not represented as real reporting, a real executive quote or a prediction of an actual NBA transaction.

## Incoming GM Calls
Formal incoming trade offers retain their existing blocking behavior but now show:
- why the other club called;
- why the included assets fit the framework;
- the other club's biggest resistance point;
- their modeled value read;
- interest, stance and philosophy context.

The user can still Accept, Counter or Decline through the existing trade workflow.

## Negotiation Rounds
User-created proposals now retain lightweight same-day negotiation context. Rejected legal frameworks increment a round counter and show the CPU team's current objection. After repeated unsuccessful frameworks, talks can cool for the rest of that league day. This avoids infinite blind offer spam while remaining reversible on the next day.

## Deadline Pressure
During the final 14 days, valuation can move slightly and directionally:
- sellers can become modestly more willing to move older players;
- buyers can pay a modest need-fit premium.

The adjustment is deliberately bounded and layers on top of the existing v0.33+ trade-value model rather than replacing it.

## Transaction Ticker
Deals/Transactions surfaces gain a compact league transaction ticker. It combines current v0.44 deadline activity with the retained transaction ledger so completed trades, signings, waivers and other recorded moves feel like part of one league wire.

## Trade Completed Presentation
A successful user trade now opens a dedicated transaction-official presentation showing:
- assets received and sent;
- salary moving each direction;
- user payroll before/after;
- roster count before/after;
- draft-asset count before/after;
- Immediate Value;
- Long-Term Value;
- Financial Impact;
- Franchise Direction Fit.

These are contextual in-save impact scores, **not letter grades** and not external media grades.

## Post-Deadline Report
For the week immediately after the deadline, the Command Center becomes a post-deadline report showing the user's deals, league transactions in the final seven-day window, current franchise stance and GM standing. The Home lead story can also become a deadline recap.

## v0.43 Integration
v0.44 preserves and feeds the v0.43 Franchise Direction + GM Evaluation layer. Completing a trade re-syncs the GM evaluation so roster-building, financial and organizational context can reflect the changed franchise state.

## Save Compatibility
Formal save schema remains **25** under `nbaCourtsideSaveV25`.

The new layer is additive under `state.tradeDeadlineV44` and initializes lazily for an existing schema-25 franchise. No destructive migration or new-franchise reset is required.
