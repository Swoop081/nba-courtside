# NBA Courtside — v0.36 Player Morale, Roles + Relationships

## Goal
Make roster management human. Players now carry role expectations, morale, coach trust, locker-room context, contract/extension outlook and promises that can create consequences over time.

## Save model
No formal save-schema bump. `nbaCourtsideSaveV25` remains the save key/schema. v0.36 adds `state.playerRelations.version = 36` with additive migration for existing saves.

## Role expectations
Each active NBA player receives a contextual season role:
- Franchise Player
- Starter
- Sixth Man
- Rotation
- Development
- Two-Way
- Depth

Role expectations are set from roster context and reset when a player changes teams or enters a new season. They are not permanent player-card labels.

## Morale model
Morale remains a 0–99 persistent score, but v0.36 expands the drivers to include:
- role / playing-time satisfaction
- winning
- contract value/security
- team direction
- stability
- coach trust
- locker-room chemistry
- recent individual form

The existing deterministic personality profile still weights how strongly different players react. Personality and generated player behavior are gameplay simulation, not factual claims about real people.

## Coach relationships
Every player has a persistent relationship anchor with the current head coach plus an adjustment that can move through role delivery, playing time and promises. Coach model emphasis and young-player willingness affect fit. Coaching changes reset the relationship toward a new baseline rather than carrying the old coach score forward unchanged.

## Locker-room chemistry
Team chemistry is a persistent daily snapshot derived from morale, winning, continuity/new arrivals, disgruntled players and trade requests. Presentation bands are Connected, Stable, Fragile and Fractured.

Locker-room grouping exposes Leaders, Young Core, Veterans, New Arrivals and Disgruntled players without turning the game into a scripted dialogue simulator.

## Front Office Conversations
Player concerns now feed one unified `Front Office Conversations` queue. Issues can be generated by playing time, coach trust, contract pressure, winning/team direction, stability or formal trade-request pressure.

Severe issues may be marked **Action Required** and block day advancement until resolved. Lower-level conversations remain visible but do not necessarily stop the calendar.

Conversation actions include, context permitting:
- Promise More Minutes
- Promise Starting Chance
- Development Plan
- No-Trade Commitment
- Commit to Playoff Push
- Open Extension Talks
- Explore Trade
- Stay the Course / Hold Firm

## Promises
Promises are persistent commitments with deadlines and evaluation rules:
- Bigger Role: target minutes over 14 days
- Starting Opportunity: starter role/minute threshold over 14 days
- Development Focus: development sessions or meaningful NBA minutes over 21 days
- No-Trade Commitment: player remains with the club for 30 days and cannot be traded by the user while active
- Playoff Push: the club must remain in a competitive direction over 30 days

Keeping a promise improves morale and coach trust. Breaking one creates a substantial morale hit, hurts coach trust and can become a media/social event.

## Trade requests
Sustained severe morale problems can generate a formal trade request. CPU trade logic already consumes the existing morale/trade-request field, so unhappy CPU players become more available in the active-front-office market rather than existing only on the user's roster.

## Extension outlook
Player profiles and the Player Relations screen now show Eager / Open / Uncertain / Reluctant / Not Interested extension outlook based on morale, role, winning, coach trust and stability. The existing CBA extension engine remains authoritative for legal contract execution. A player who is Not Interested can refuse an otherwise legal extension; Reluctant players can also decline talks.

## Presentation
v0.36 adds a dedicated Player Relations screen, Daily Hub conversation module, roster portal, enhanced player-profile relationship panel, active promises, locker-room groups, relationship history and simulated player reactions in Courtside Social.

All generated dialogue/reactions are labeled simulated and are not real quotes.

## Unchanged foundations
v0.36 does not retune v0.29 ratings, NBA/G League Game Day simulation rates, NBA schedule/Cup, CBA, roster source data, future picks, G League or College + Draft data.
